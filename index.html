Foundational & Responsible AI

I am an incoming PhD student in Computer Science at Purdue University, working under Dr. Aniket Bera in the IDEAS Lab. My research broadly focuses on foundational multimodal responsible AI, with an emphasis on building robust, and generalizable models.

During my undergraduate studies at IIIT Delhi (B.Tech CS, 2021–2025), I worked on multimodal emotion understanding, physiological signal modeling, and real-world data collection frameworks with Prof. Pushpendra Singh and Prof. Mohan Kumar. I also worked on scholarly recommendation systems using structured representations and graph-based methods with Prof. Mukesh Mohania. This work led to publications at NeurIPS 2024 & 2025, ACM IMWUT 2025, and ACM India HCI 2023.

I have also briefly worked on applied machine learning systems and scalable ML pipelines for time-series modeling in the fintech domain.

I am interested in research collaborations and summer internships.

Selected Research

Published in A* venues. Filter by theme below. Full list on Google Scholar ↗

NeurIPS 2025
FEEL: Quantifying Heterogeneity in Physiological Signals for Generalizable Emotion Recognition
A large-scale benchmarking framework for emotion recognition from physiological signals, unifying evaluation across 19 EDA and PPG datasets. FEEL benchmarks 16 architectures across traditional, deep, and self-supervised paradigms under both within- and cross-dataset settings, revealing strong generalization of contrastive signal-language pretraining and the continued robustness of feature-based models in low-resource environments.
NeurIPS 2025 Project Page
NeurIPS 2024
EEVR: A Dataset of Paired Physiological Signals and Textual Descriptions for Joint Emotion Representation Learning
A multimodal dataset pairing EDA and PPG signals from 360° VR-based emotion elicitation with participant-level textual descriptions (37 subjects), enabling context-aware emotion recognition. Introduces contrastive language-signal pretraining (CLSP), improving performance and enabling strong zero-shot transfer across datasets.
NeurIPS Project Page
ACM IMWUT 2025 · UbiComp
AnnoSense: Navigating the Complexities of Collecting and Annotating Everyday Emotion Data for AI
A stakeholder-informed framework for real-world emotion data collection, developed through studies with 119 participants including the public, clinicians, and AI researchers. Produces 15 actionable guidelines for reliable, usable, and ethically grounded emotion annotation, validated by domain experts.
ACM DL UbiComp
Under Review
CITAR: Citation Inference using Taxonomy-Aligned Retrieval and Context-Aware Entity Matching
A context-aware citation recommendation system that aligns hierarchical academic taxonomies to generate relevant citations with precise insertion points. Achieves strong retrieval performance (Recall@10 0.35, MRR 0.21) while improving exposure balance across the corpus, demonstrating effective trade-offs between relevance, efficiency, and fairness.
Under Review

Toward Inclusive NLP: A Survey of LGBTQIA+ Representation and Gaps in Language Technologies
A systematic survey of 86 NLP papers examining the relationship between language technologies and LGBTQIA+ communities. Identifies key gaps in proactive bias mitigation, intersectional representation, and non-English settings, highlighting opportunities for building more inclusive and equitable NLP systems.
